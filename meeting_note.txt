The rhyme helper accepts a seed word and checks a short set of practice lines. It looks up dictionary rhymes, keeps words found in a small local word bank and lists their syllable counts. Two short functions handle word counting and line analysis. Each practice line receives a visible word-by-word breakdown, a total and a comparison with the chosen syllable target. The program also compares the ending sounds of paired lines. For the supplied light example, it returns twelve rhyme suggestions and counts eight syllables in each line. Results are saved as a readable session and a tab-separated word list. The lines are supplied examples, not generated verses. Corpus selection and Markov-based line generation remain the next steps towards the A1 proposal.
